Half-Page Reflection: Debugging & Iteration

During the initial build of the Salon Queue Management System, one key issue the AI agent got wrong was barber-preference isolation and wait-time recalculation during the Call next workflow. In an early pass, the agent treated Call next as a simple First-In-First-Out (FIFO) pop from the front of the queue, which meant clicking Call next for Barber Amit could mistakenly assign a customer who had explicitly requested Barber Ravi. Additionally, when a barber was toggled to On break, the estimated wait time did not immediately adjust because the divisor still counted all on-duty barbers rather than only available working barbers.

I noticed both issues while testing side-by-side browser windows using the verification scenarios in docs/03-flows-rules.md. When I created a test customer locked to Ravi ahead of a customer who chose Any, clicking Call next on Amit pulled the wrong customer, and toggling Imran to On break left the Home screen's estimated wait unchanged.

To fix this cleanly, I updated docs/03-flows-rules.md and AGENTS.md to enforce a single-source-of-truth rule (src/lib/queueEngine.js) and explicitly defined both constraints: (1) Call next for barber X must scan in FIFO order for the first waiting entry where preferredBarber === X || preferredBarber === 'Any', and (2) the wait-time divisor must count only barbers where onDuty && !onBreak. Re-prompting against the updated specification resolved both bugs across every screen without altering the UI.
